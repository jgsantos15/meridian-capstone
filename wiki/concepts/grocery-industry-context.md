# Grocery Industry Context

National grocery-sector trends and competitive dynamics relevant to Meridian's specialty-grocery positioning and future site-selection decisions.

**Sources:** `raw/icsc-grocery-sector-trends` (Commerce + Communities Today, April 24, 2026)

## Market size & investment appetite
- The U.S. grocery sector is valued at **$912.4 billion** (IBISWorld).
- Deals for grocery-anchored centers totaled **almost $11 billion in 2025, up 42% from 2024** (JLL Grocery Tracker 2026).
- Naveen Jaggi (JLL): "The days when any grocery store could anchor a center equally well are fading" — specialty and value-oriented formats are reshaping the landscape, not generic grocery anchors.

## Specialty/value bifurcation — where Meridian sits
- Trader Joe's grew **3% year-over-year**, 6 points above the sector average, with "double-digit year-over-year growth among Gen Z shoppers" (Consumer Edge).
- Traditional grocers (Publix, Safeway) are "seeing pullbacks from every income group, with the sharpest declines among lower-income shoppers" (Consumer Edge).
- Discount grocery (Aldi, Food 4 Less) growth has **plateaued since mid-2025**.
- Michael Gunther (Consumer Edge): shoppers gravitate to retailers offering "a clear reason to be loyal, whether that's unbeatable value at a hard discounter or a curated private-label experience." Meridian's stated positioning — prepared foods, local sourcing, smaller footprint than national chains — reads as a bet on the "curated" side of that split, not the discount side.

## A close comparable: Gelson's (Southern California)
- Gelson's operates **27 traditional grocery stores in Southern California** — the closest scale/geography comparable to Meridian's 14 stores across LA/OC/Ventura.
- Gelson's is piloting small-format "ReCharge by Gelson's" micromarkets (3,000–5,400 sq. ft.) paired with EV charging, in Costa Mesa and Santa Ana.
- Worth understanding in the interview whether Dana considers Gelson's a direct competitor or a format Meridian is watching.

## Restaurant-vs-grocery substitution
- Per Tillster's 2026 Phygital Index Report (2,144 diners surveyed): 36% visit grocery stores more often and 33% visit convenience stores more often, while fast-food visits are down for 29% of respondents and fast-casual visits are down for 37%. Grocery — prepared foods especially — appears to be picking up share from restaurant spend, which lines up directly with Meridian's prepared-foods positioning.

## Investment activity in Meridian's own markets
- Brixton Capital acquired Quad at Whittier (Vallarta Supermarkets-anchored, Los Angeles) for **$100 million** (314,593 sq. ft., 95.6% leased) — a grocery-anchored center in one of Meridian's own counties, signaling continued investor appetite for grocery-anchored retail in the LA market specifically.
